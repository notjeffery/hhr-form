Emergency Support Application

Helping students remain enrolled, stable, and focused during times of unexpected hardship.

The Student Resilience Fund provides limited emergency financial assistance to eligible college students experiencing urgent financial challenges that may interfere with their education, housing stability, health, transportation, or basic needs.

Please complete this application honestly and provide as much detail as possible. Applications are reviewed based on available funding, level of need, and program eligibility.

Warning: Providing false or misleading information on this form constitutes fraud and will lead to automatic disqualification from all aid.
