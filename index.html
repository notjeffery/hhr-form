About the Fund

The Student Resilience Fund is an emergency assistance initiative of The Household Resilience Initiative designed to help college students and young adults (up to age 23) overcome unexpected financial barriers that place their education, housing, or well-being at risk.

We believe that no student should be forced to abandon their education because of a temporary financial crisis. Through timely assistance and supportive services, we help students remain focused on their academic goals while navigating life’s unexpected challenges.

What We May Assist With

Eligible students may receive support for essential emergency needs, including:

  • Housing Stability – overdue rent, security deposits, temporary emergency lodging, and utility assistance.
  • Food & Basic Needs – groceries, meal assistance, hygiene products, and household essentials.
  • Transportation – gas vouchers, public transportation passes, emergency vehicle repairs, or travel for essential appointments.
  • Medical & Wellness – prescriptions, emergency medical expenses, counseling, vision, or dental care.
  • Academic Essentials – textbooks, laptops, required course materials, internet access, and equipment needed to continue studies.
  • Safety & Family Support – emergency childcare, relocation assistance related to safety concerns, and replacement of essential belongings following theft or disaster.

Simple, Student-Centered Process

Financial emergencies cannot always wait.

Applications are accepted year-round and are reviewed as quickly as possible. Our goal is to provide timely assistance so students can return their focus to learning rather than worrying about immediate financial barriers.

Whenever appropriate, payments may be made directly to landlords, schools, service providers, bookstores, or other verified vendors.

Eligibility

Applicants may qualify if they:

  • Are 18–23 years of age.
  • Are currently enrolled in an accredited college, university, technical, trade, or vocational program (minimum half-time enrollment).
  • Are experiencing a documented financial hardship affecting their ability to continue their education.
  • Meet the program’s financial eligibility requirements.

Priority may be given to students facing housing instability, food insecurity, medical emergencies, family emergencies, or circumstances that place their education at immediate risk.

Beyond Emergency Assistance

Emergency funding is only one part of student success.

Whenever possible, participants are connected with additional resources including financial literacy coaching, financial aid guidance, career readiness support, mental health resources, academic success services, and community partners that can help strengthen long-term stability.

Our goal isn’t simply to help students overcome today’s emergency—it’s to help them stay enrolled, complete their education, and build a stronger future.
